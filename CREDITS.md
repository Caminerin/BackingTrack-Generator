Attribution is required by their licenses and must remain with the app.

- **Piano** — Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (retuned by Markus
  Fiedler). License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC-BY 3.0).
  https://github.com/Caminerin/SalamanderGrandPiano
- **Bass / Guitar** — FreePats (CC0-1.0). https://freepats.zenvoid.org/
- **Drums** — Virtuosity Drums, a contemporary jazz kit performed by Austin
  McMahon and recorded at Virtuosity Musical Instruments (Boston, MA) by
  DSmolken / Karoryfer Samples. License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
  (CC-BY 3.0). https://github.com/Caminerin/virtuosity_drums

Per-sample source and license metadata is also recorded in
`app/src/main/assets/packs/core/manifest.json`.
